Questions

The honest answers.

Where does processing happen?

On your computer. VideoDoc does not upload your video to a processing server.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free transcribes local files in your browser. Pro adds links, playlists, screenshots, long videos, more exports, and downloading.

Can it capture slides instead of the presenter?

Yes. It keeps useful screen changes such as slides, code, and diagrams, while dropping duplicates.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes — Windows 10/11 and Apple Silicon Macs are supported (Mac guide). More answers on the support page.
