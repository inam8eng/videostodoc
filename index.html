Questions

The honest answers.

The video is a person talking over slides. What do I get?

VideoDoc follows the screen share, not the face. The screenshots capture the slides, code and diagrams, paired with the timestamped transcript.

Where does the processing happen?

Entirely on your own machine. The video is downloaded, transcribed and captured locally. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. VideoDoc auto-detects your GPU and CPU and tunes itself; it uses your graphics card when it can, and still works fine without one.

Is there a subscription?

No. Free is free forever in your browser, and Pro is one payment you keep for life.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free transcribes your own local files in the browser. Pro adds links and playlists from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, the on-screen screenshots, long videos, the HD/4K downloader, and use on two machines.

Does it work with private or paywalled videos?

Links work for public videos only. For anything private or behind a login, save the file yourself and drop it in; it never leaves your computer.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. It runs on Windows 10/11 and Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer), all processing local, with a one-time 10-second unlock on the Mac's first launch (guide here).

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. VideoDoc for Android starts free with 3 downloads and 3 text extractions, then one small one-time unlock; this page updates the day it lands on Google Play.
