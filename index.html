Questions

The honest answers.

The video is a person talking over slides. What do I get?

VideoDoc follows the screen share, not the face. The screenshots capture the slides, code and diagrams, paired with the timestamped transcript.

Where does the processing happen?

Entirely on your own machine. The video is downloaded, transcribed and captured locally. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. VideoDoc auto-detects your GPU and CPU and tunes itself; it uses your graphics card when it can, and still works fine without one.

Is there a subscription?

No. Free is free forever in your browser. Pro is a single one-time payment. Buy once, keep it forever. The Android unlock, when it arrives, is one-time too.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free transcribes your own local files in the browser. Pro adds links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, plus playlists, on-screen screenshots, long videos, HD/4K download and use on two machines.

Does it work with private or paywalled videos?

Links work for public videos. Anything private, behind a login or a paywall will not download, and that is by design. For those, use the file route: save or export the video yourself, then drop the file in. It never leaves your computer.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. VideoDoc runs on Windows 10/11 and on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer; Intel Macs are not supported), with all processing on your own hardware. On a Mac the very first launch has a one-time 10 second unlock step, with a copy-button guide at videostodoc.com/mac.

Is there an Android app?

It's on the way. VideoDoc for Android starts free, with 3 video downloads and 3 text extractions on the house, then one small one-time unlock opens unlimited use. It isn't on Google Play yet; this page will update the day it lands.
