Turn one long video into short clips.

Video to shorts is a tab inside VideoDoc. It finds the best moments in a long video or stream, plays each one on a phone preview, and exports vertical clips with word by word captions, ready for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels.

How it works

1

Add a video

Drop a video on the tab, or paste a public link or a file path. A file path makes no copy, which is best for big files.

2

Press Make shorts

VideoDoc listens to the video on your computer, notes the time of every word, finds every scene cut and the faces, and measures how loud each moment is. Then it suggests moments in four lengths: up to 30 seconds, 30 to 60 seconds, 1 to 2 minutes and 2 to 3 minutes.

3

Check it, then export

A big phone in the middle plays your short exactly as it will look, captions and all. Trim it, join parts, and press Export.

Moments that start in the right place

Every suggestion starts where a sentence starts and ends where a sentence or a clear pause ends, so you do not get a short that begins in the middle of a thought.

Each one also tells you why it was picked, in plain words: it starts with a question, it is a complete thought, or the voice is lively. For gameplay or music with almost no talking, VideoDoc uses the sound and the scene cuts instead.

Simple to edit

One timeline shows the time on a ruler and pictures of the video, so you always know where you are. Drag the thick handles to trim, drag across an empty spot to pick your own part, or type the start and end. Join several parts into one short. The edges snap to whole words, so no word is cut in half.

Open the Words tab and select the words you want: they become your short. Undo and Redo take back any change.

Keep the person in view

Auto framing follows the speaker's face and keeps it inside the frame. When nobody is on screen, you see the whole picture on a blurred background.

For a screen recording or gameplay with a webcam, the facecam goes on top and the whole screen below, so no text on your screen is cut off. Or pick Your frame, drag it to where the person is and pull a corner to zoom in. The phone shows the result while you do it, and the export matches it.

Captions and export

Word by word captions are on by default, with the spoken word highlighted, in three styles: Bold, Clean and Big.

Export vertical 9:16 at 1080x1920, or square, 4:5, or the original shape, at the frame rate of your video (at least 24 frames a second). Export one short, or tick several parts and export them all.

Good to know

The first time, VideoDoc downloads its speech engine to your computer (486 MB, about 3 minutes on a normal home connection). A blue box tells you the size, shows the MB done and the time left, and you can cancel and carry on later.

Links must be public. YouTube sometimes refuses the first try. If it does, press Retry. For a private or paywalled video, use the file on your computer.

Only clip videos you made or have the right to use. If the video is someone else's, ask first and read the rules of the creator and of the platform you post on.
