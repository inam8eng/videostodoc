Privacy Policy: VideoDoc for Android

Last updated: 8 November 2026

VideoDoc does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There is no account and no sign-up. The only thing that can ever leave your phone through us is the link to a YouTube video, in one case explained below, and that link is not stored.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no tracking of any kind. We do not know who you are, that you installed the app, or what you do with it.

Uninstalling the app removes your settings. Files you have already saved stay on your phone, because they are yours.

Network connections

When you paste a link and tap a button, your device connects directly to the website that link points to, in order to fetch the media. It is the same connection your browser would make. Apart from the one YouTube case below, nothing you paste is sent to us or to anyone else.

A few more connections can happen, and none carries anything about you:

  • When a video has no subtitles, the app downloads a speech model file once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), so it can listen to the video on your phone. The listening itself happens on your phone.
  • Play Store version, YouTube videos with no subtitles only. When you ask for the text of a YouTube video that has no subtitles, the app sends that video's link, and nothing else, to a small relay of ours (a Cloudflare Worker). The relay passes the link to Google's Gemini API, which listens to the public video and sends the words back to your phone. The relay stores nothing and keeps no logs. Google receives the link under its own Gemini API terms, and on the free tier Google may use what it receives to improve its products. No account, name, device ID or file is sent. The website version never does this; it listens on your phone.
  • When a YouTube link's page cannot be read, the Play Store version may ask YouTube's public oEmbed address for the video's title, so the file gets a proper name.
  • If you buy the unlock, Google Play (Play Store version) or Gumroad (website version) handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

Clipboard

If you leave "Paste links automatically" switched on, the app reads your clipboard when you bring it to the foreground, so it can fill in the link box for you. What it reads is used immediately to populate that box on your screen and is never stored, logged or transmitted. You can switch this off in Settings at any time, and paste manually instead.
