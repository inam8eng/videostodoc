How to turn a Facebook video into text, and why a private group video will not work

Inam Ul Haq·Updated October 28, 2026·7 min read
The short answer

To turn a Facebook video into text, switch on Always Show Captions in your settings first, because some videos already have captions attached. Facebook does not give you a button to copy those captions out as text. If you want the full transcript, paste the link of a public video or reel into a tool that downloads it and listens to the audio. Videos from private groups or friends-only posts need a login, so no link tool can reach them.

Last Updated: October 13, 2026.

You found a useful video on Facebook, like a recipe, a talk from a page you follow or an explainer in a group, and you want to read it as text. Most articles about this are written by transcription businesses, and they tell you to copy the link and paste it into their site. That works for some public videos, but it fails on many others, and those guides do not tell you why.

If the video is public, a link tool can turn it into text. If you can only see it because you are logged in, as a friend or a group member, no link tool can reach it, mine included. I will explain why this happens, and what you can do about it.

Does Facebook already show captions I can read?

You can see captions sometimes. Facebook has a setting that shows captions on any video that includes them. On a desktop computer, click your profile picture, go to Settings and privacy, open Settings, select Media, and turn on Always Show Captions. On an iPhone, it is a phone setting under Accessibility, then Subtitles and Captioning, then Closed Captions + SDH. You can check these steps on Facebook's own captions help page.

That same page explains that not every video on Facebook comes with captions. They are there when the person who posted the video added them, for example by uploading a subtitle file, which is an SRT file containing the spoken text along with timing markers. Even when captions exist, Facebook displays them one line at a time on top of the video, and there is no button to copy them as a transcript.

Why is every Facebook video a reel now?

This setup confuses many people, so a line of background helps. In June 2025 Meta announced that all Facebook videos would be shared as reels, with no length limit, and that the Video tab would be renamed to Reels. You can read their announcement in Making it Easier to Create Videos on Facebook. Videos uploaded before that change stayed where they were.

When you open a video today, its web address often contains /reel/ even if it is a 40 minute presentation. This address change does not affect turning the video into text. A reel is just a video, and whether a tool can access it depends on who is allowed to watch it.

Which Facebook videos can a tool actually reach?

Any software that turns a web link into text must fetch the video file first, just like your browser does when you hit play. A tool with only the link acts like a visitor who is not logged into Facebook. If a logged-out visitor can watch the video, the tool can usually fetch it too, but if you need to be a friend or a group member to view it, the tool gets blocked.

Private groups confuse people most often. Since you are a member, you can play the video, so it seems like the link should work. But that link only opens because Facebook knows your account. If you give that link to anyone else or to an automated tool, Facebook demands a login.

To get a link that works, click the Share button under the video and select Copy link. Do not copy the address from your browser address bar while scrolling through the feed, because the Share button gives you a direct link to that specific video.

What is the free way to get the text?

Facebook allows you to download the reels you shared yourself using their mobile app, but they do not let you download reels from other accounts. So the free option you choose depends on who owns the video.

  1. Turn on Always Show Captions and play the video. If captions appear and you only need a few lines, read or copy them by hand.
  2. If it is your own video, download it from the Facebook app on your phone, or ask the person who posted it to send you the file.
  3. Put that file into a free transcriber that runs on your computer. OpenAI's Whisper is the usual one: it listens to the speech and writes it down, and once it is installed it needs no internet.
  4. If it is someone else's public video and you have no file, you are left with a link tool.

Free methods have clear trade-offs. Whisper is a command line tool, so you must type text commands instead of clicking buttons, and a slow computer will take a long time to process an hour of speech. It also only processes audio, which means any text on screen like a recipe, a slide or a price will not appear in your transcript.

Quick questions

Can I get a transcript of a Facebook video?

Facebook does not offer a transcript download button. You can enable Always Show Captions to read subtitles over the video when the author uploaded them. To get full text, download your own file to transcribe it, or use a tool that processes the audio from a public video link.

Why can't I transcribe a video from a private Facebook group?

Videos in private groups only play for members who are logged in. An external tool opening a plain link is not logged into your account, so Facebook redirects it to a login page instead of playing the file. You will need to ask the video creator to send you the original file.

Does Facebook make automatic captions for every video?

No, it does not. Facebook's own help page states that not all videos on Facebook have captions. When captions are missing, turning on Always Show Captions does nothing, and you need a tool that listens to the audio.

Does it matter that my Facebook video is a reel?

It makes no difference for extracting text. Starting in 2025, Facebook began publishing all new videos as reels regardless of length. The only factor that matters is whether the video is public or restricted to friends and groups.

Check who can view the Facebook video you want to convert. If it is marked public, you can turn it into a readable document today.
