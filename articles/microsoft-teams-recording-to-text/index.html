How to turn a Teams meeting recording into text, and what to do when you cannot download it

Inam Ul Haq·Updated October 20, 2026·7 min read
The short answer

A Teams recording only has a transcript if transcription was running during the meeting. If there is none, you make the text yourself: download the .mp4 from the Recordings folder in the organizer's OneDrive and open it in a transcription tool on your own computer. If you see no download option, ask the organizer for the file, because by default attendees can watch a recording but not download it.

You were in a Teams meeting, someone recorded it, and now you want the words. You open the recording from the meeting chat and it plays fine, but there is no transcript, and when you look for a way to save the video there is no download option either. Nothing is broken on your side.

Teams decides what you get by two things: whether transcription was running in the meeting, and whether you were the organizer. If there is no transcript, you have to make the text from the .mp4 file. If you cannot download that file, ask the organizer to send it to you. The rest of this page explains both, and how to keep the shared screen, which a Teams transcript never has.

Does Teams make a transcript of every recording?

No, because Teams writes its transcript while the meeting is happening, and a transcript only exists if transcription was switched on at the time. Microsoft's live transcription help page says recording a meeting starts transcription automatically, but your IT admin's meeting policy has to allow it. If the policy turns transcription off, the recording arrives with nothing to read.

When a transcript does exist, it is good. It is saved in the organizer's OneDrive next to the recording, it can be downloaded as a .docx or a .vtt file, and it shows each speaker's name with a time stamp. VTT is a captions format, which means plain lines of text with a start and end time on each line. By default only the organizer and co-organizers can download it, and anyone else depends on what the admin allows.

Where is a Teams recording saved, and how do I download it?

For a normal meeting, the recording goes into a folder called Recordings in the organizer's OneDrive, even if the organizer never joined the call. A channel meeting is different, and its recording goes to the team's SharePoint site, into a Recordings folder inside the channel's documents. A one to one call or a group call goes to the OneDrive of whoever pressed Record. Microsoft lays all of this out on its recording storage page.

  1. Find the recording in the meeting chat, or in the channel conversation for a channel meeting.
  2. Open it in OneDrive or SharePoint in your web browser, rather than just playing it inside Teams.
  3. Use Download in the bar at the top and save the .mp4 somewhere easy, like your Downloads folder.

Two things are worth knowing before you start. Microsoft puts a one hour recording at about 400 MB, so a long workshop is a heavy download on a slow connection. And your admin can set recordings to expire after a while, so get the file while it is still there instead of planning to do it next month.

Why can't I download the Teams recording?

Usually because you were not the organizer. In a scheduled meeting the recording belongs to the organizer, the organizer and co-organizers can edit and share it, and everyone else from the same company can watch it. Microsoft's recording help page says that by default only organizers and co-organizers can download it. On top of that, an admin can block downloads of recordings for the whole company.

So the fix is to ask the organizer to send you the .mp4. It takes them a minute from their own Recordings folder. Do not try to get around a blocked download with a screen recorder, because if the company turned downloads off it did that on purpose, and the recording has other people's faces and voices in it.

What is the free way to turn the Teams .mp4 into text?

OpenAI's Whisper is free and runs on your own computer, and nothing is uploaded. You use it from a terminal, which is the window where you type commands to your computer instead of clicking buttons. It needs Python and ffmpeg installed first, and the setup is explained on that page.

  1. Put the downloaded recording in its own folder and open a terminal there.
  2. Type whisper "meeting.mp4" --model small --output_format txt with the real name of your file.
  3. Leave it running, because Whisper has to listen to the whole meeting, so an hour of recording takes a while on an ordinary laptop.
  4. Open the .txt file that appears beside the recording when it finishes.

What you get is the words and nothing else. There are no speaker names, which the Teams transcript would have had, and nothing from the shared screen. That second part matters more than people expect in a work meeting.

How do I keep the shared screen as well as the words?

Most Teams meetings are half talking and half screen sharing. Someone puts up the project plan, the budget sheet or the slide with the numbers, and then talks over it. A transcript keeps a line like "the red rows are the ones we are late on" and throws away the red rows, which were the reason you wanted the recording.

Honest limits

VideoDoc does not label who said what, and the Teams transcript does. If Teams made a transcript and you only need the words, its .docx is the better file. VideoDoc also cannot open a Teams, OneDrive or SharePoint link, because those sit behind your work sign in, so it needs the .mp4 on your computer. How long a job takes depends on your computer and the length of the meeting.

Quick questions

Can I get a Teams transcript after the meeting has ended?

Not from Teams, if transcription was not running during the meeting, because Teams makes its transcript live. You can still make one afterwards from the recording's .mp4 with a tool on your own computer.

Where are Teams meeting recordings saved?

A normal meeting's recording goes to the Recordings folder in the organizer's OneDrive. A channel meeting's recording goes to a Recordings folder on the team's SharePoint site, and a call goes to the OneDrive of whoever pressed Record.

Why is there no download option on my Teams recording?

By default only the organizer and co-organizers can download a meeting recording, and an admin can block downloads completely. Everyone else from the company can usually watch it, so ask the organizer to send you the .mp4.

Does a Teams transcript show who is speaking?

Yes. A Teams transcript shows each speaker's name with a time stamp, unless a person chose to hide their identity in captions and transcripts. A transcript you make yourself from the .mp4 with Whisper or VideoDoc does not name the speakers.
