How to turn a Webex recording into text, even when Webex made no transcript

Inam Ul Haq·Updated October 31, 2026·7 min read
The short answer

Webex only writes a transcript for an MP4 cloud recording, and only when the site admin has transcripts switched on. Even then it can skip one when the audio is noisy or not in English. If you have no transcript, get the MP4 from the host, or from User Hub if you are the host, and turn it into text on your own computer. A local recording never gets a Webex transcript.

You were sent a Webex recording link after a meeting, and it plays fine. But there is no transcript panel beside it, or the panel only shows an error. You wanted the words so you could write the minutes, or paste them into an AI and ask what was actually agreed. Nothing is broken on your side.

The surprising part is that Webex decides on its own whether a transcript gets made. If there is no transcript, get the MP4 file and turn it into text yourself. Below I explain who is allowed to get that file, why Webex leaves some recordings without text, and how to keep the shared screen, which the Webex transcript never has.

Does Webex make a transcript of every recording?

No, and there are three separate reasons. First, transcripts only exist for recordings saved in the cloud. Cisco's recording transcripts page says administrators turn on automatic transcription for MP4 meeting recordings, either for every host or only for some. So whether you get one depends on how your company set up its Webex site, not on anything you clicked.

Second, the same page says the transcripts support English audio. Third, and this is the one that confuses people, Webex will not produce a transcript at all if it expects the result to be poor. It names a noisy background, poor acoustics, non-English words in the audio and thick accents. So a call with people speaking two languages, or one person on a bad headset in a busy office, can come back with an error in the panel instead of text.

Timing matters too. Cisco says the transcript usually appears within about twice the length of the meeting, and in rare cases it can take up to 24 hours. A one hour meeting recorded this morning may simply not have its text yet.

Where is a Webex recording saved, and who can download it?

Webex has two kinds of recording, and Cisco's Record a meeting page says your plan decides which one you can use. Cloud recording is not available on the free plan. It works on the Starter, Plus and Business plans, and on enterprise plans it is whatever the site administrator configured. Recording to your own computer works on the free, Starter, Plus and Business plans, and on the free plan only from the desktop app.

Both kinds save as MP4. A cloud recording goes to the host's Webex site, and the host finds it in User Hub under Recordings, where they can play, download and share it. Cisco says it can take up to 24 hours to arrive. A local recording just sits in a folder on the computer of the person who pressed Record, and it leaves out the chat and participants panels.

If you were only invited, Cisco's own advice is to ask the host for the recording, and for its link and password. There is one exception worth knowing. If the meeting was linked to a space in the Webex App, everyone in that space is treated as the host and can open the recording. If you can watch it but not download it, ask the host to send you the MP4. It takes them a minute, and it is far better than screen recording something they chose to keep locked.

What is the free way to turn a Webex recording into text?

OpenAI's Whisper is free, runs on your own computer and never uploads the file. You use it from a terminal, which is just a window where you type commands instead of clicking buttons. It needs Python and ffmpeg installed first, and its page explains how.

  1. Put the MP4 in a folder of its own and open a terminal in that folder.
  2. Type whisper "meeting.mp4" --model small --output_format txt, with your real file name.
  3. Let it run. It listens to the whole meeting, so an hour of audio takes a while on an ordinary laptop.
  4. Open the new .txt file that appears next to the video.

This also works on the calls Webex refused to transcribe, because Whisper understands many languages, not only English. But it gives you the spoken words and nothing else. It does not know who was speaking, and it cannot see the screen.

How do I keep the shared screen, not just the words?

Cisco says a cloud recording includes the audio, the video and anything shared during the meeting. So the slides, the spreadsheet and the network diagram are all inside the MP4. The transcript only has the words, and that is a real problem in a work meeting, where someone says "this row here is the one that failed" and the row is only on the screen.

Honest limits

VideoDoc needs the MP4 on your computer, so it cannot open a Webex link that sits behind a sign in or a password, and it cannot help with a recording the host will not share. It does not tell you who is speaking. If Webex already made a good transcript and you only want the words, use that, it is quicker. On a computer without a graphics card, turning an hour of audio into text takes a fair while.

Quick questions

Why does my Webex recording have no transcript?

Either your site admin has not switched on recording transcripts, or it was a local recording, which never gets one. Webex also skips the transcript when it expects poor results, for example noisy audio, heavy accents or words that are not English.

How long does a Webex transcript take?

Cisco says it usually appears within about twice the length of the meeting. In rare cases it can take up to 24 hours, so check again later before you give up on it.

Can I download a Webex recording if I was not the host?

Usually you need the host to share the recording link and its password, or to send you the file. If the meeting was linked to a space in the Webex App, everyone in the space is treated as the host and can open it.

Can I record a Webex meeting on the free plan?

Yes, but only to your own computer and only from the desktop app. Cloud recording, and with it the Webex transcript, needs a paid plan.
