Get the transcript of any YouTube video, even without captions

Inam Ul Haq·Updated July 18, 2026·5 min read
The short answer

If the video has captions: three dots under the video, Show transcript, copy. If it does not, or the captions are garbage, transcribe the audio itself: VideoDoc downloads the video on your own computer and writes every word with timestamps, in many languages, no captions required.

The video that finally broke me was an Urdu lecture with auto captions that read like a prank. Half the words were wrong and the other half were in the wrong script. YouTube's transcript button is a gift when it works, and a wall when it does not, so you need both methods in your pocket.

Method 1: the transcript is already there

  1. Open the video on YouTube in a browser.
  2. Under the title, click the three dots, then Show transcript.
  3. The panel lists every line with its timestamp. Toggle timestamps off from the small menu if you want prose only.
  4. Select everything, copy, and paste into your notes.

When the creator uploaded proper subtitles this text is genuinely good. When it says auto-generated, treat every technical word with suspicion, because the caption engine guesses from sound alone.

Method 2: transcribe the audio, skip captions entirely

This is what I do for anything that matters now. VideoDoc takes the link, downloads the video over your own connection, and runs AI transcription on your own machine. The result is every word with timestamps, whether the video has captions or not, because it never looks at captions in the first place.

  • Pick the spoken language before you start. It handles many, including Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Russian, Chinese and Japanese, with English the strongest today.
  • Choose the Best engine setting when accuracy matters more than speed.
  • Long videos are fine. 3 hour lectures are the sweet spot, and a safety timer on every step means it never hangs overnight.

You get the transcript inside a clean PDF and Markdown document, and if the video shows slides or code, those come along as images too. Nothing uploads anywhere at any point.

Quick questions

Why does a YouTube video have no transcript button?

Either the creator turned captions off, the language is not supported by YouTube's caption engine, or the video is too new. Transcribing the audio with a tool like VideoDoc works regardless, because it does not use captions at all.

Can I get a transcript with timestamps?

Yes. YouTube's transcript panel shows timestamps for captioned videos, and VideoDoc writes timestamps on every block for any video, so you can jump back to the exact moment.

Does this work for languages like Urdu or Hindi?

Yes. VideoDoc transcribes many languages including Hindi, Urdu and Arabic on your own machine. Pick the spoken language and the Best engine quality for the cleanest result.
