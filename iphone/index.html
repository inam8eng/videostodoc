VideoDoc on your iPhone.

The full version. Save videos and turn them into text and documents from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and many more sites. Apple does not allow apps like this in the App Store, so you install it yourself with a free helper on your computer. It takes about ten minutes the first time.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or newer.
  • A Windows PC or a Mac, and your iPhone's cable.
  • Your Apple ID (a free one is fine).
  • The file VideoDoc-1.0.0.ipa from this page.

Way 1: Sideloadly (easiest on Windows)

1

Get Sideloadly

Download and install Sideloadly from sideloadly.io. On Windows it also asks for Apple's iTunes and iCloud from apple.com (not the Microsoft Store versions).

2

Plug in your iPhone

Unlock it and tap Trust when it asks.

3

Install the file

Open Sideloadly, drag VideoDoc-1.0.0.ipa into it, type your Apple ID and press Start.

4

Trust it on the iPhone

Open Settings, General, VPN and Device Management, tap your Apple ID and tap Trust.

5

Turn on Developer Mode, once

On iOS 16 and newer: Settings, Privacy and Security, Developer Mode, then restart. After that, open VideoDoc.

Way 2: AltStore (refreshes by itself over Wi-Fi)

1

Put AltStore on your iPhone

Install AltServer from altstore.io on your computer and use it to put AltStore on your iPhone.

2

Add VideoDoc

Open the file in AltStore on the iPhone, or tap the plus button in My Apps.

3

Keep AltServer running

Keep AltServer running on your computer and the phone on the same Wi-Fi, and AltStore refreshes VideoDoc for you.

The 7 day rule

With a free Apple ID, Apple lets apps you install this way run for 7 days. After that VideoDoc will not open until you refresh it: AltStore does it by itself over Wi-Fi, or press Start in Sideloadly again. Your files stay on the phone. With a paid Apple Developer account the app runs for a year.

A free Apple ID has two more limits from Apple: at most 3 apps installed this way at the same time (AltStore itself counts as one), and at most 10 app IDs in 7 days. VideoDoc uses two of those (the app and its Share button), so installing many different apps in one week can make Apple refuse the next one for a few days.

To update VideoDoc, install the new file over the old one with the same Apple ID. Your files and your key stay. Deleting the app deletes its files, so save anything you need to the Files app or Photos first.

Good to know

  • Keep VideoDoc open while it works. iPhone pauses apps in the background, and VideoDoc carries on when you come back.
  • Your files are in the Files app, under On My iPhone, VideoDoc. Saved videos can also go to Photos.
  • Videos with no captions are listened to on your phone. The first time, VideoDoc downloads its 78 MB speech model.
  • YouTube videos save in the best MP4 quality, usually up to 1080p.
  • Your videos and documents are never uploaded. VideoDoc goes online only to fetch the video from its site (like a browser), to check your key with Gumroad, to look for updates, and once to download its speech model.
  • Unlock Pro with the same VideoDoc key you bought for the desktop app. A key works on 2 devices in all, computers and iPhones together. Activating the same iPhone again, even after reinstalling with the same Apple ID and the same helper (Sideloadly or AltStore), never uses up another. A different Apple ID counts as a new device.
  • Signing in to YouTube inside the app may be refused by Google. Most public videos need no sign in.
  • Only save videos you made or have the right to use.
